About the App

Dialog Master helps users explore communication techniques inspired by movie scenes. The app analyzes selected dialogue moments to highlight useful strategies for real-life communication.

Privacy Policy

Dialog Master does not collect personal data without your consent. Any analysis generated by the app stays on your device unless you choose to share it. We do not store images, audio, or dialogue content on external servers.

The app may use third-party services for analytics or crash reporting. These services operate under their own privacy guidelines.
